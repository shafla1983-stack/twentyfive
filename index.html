    How to play

    Everyone gets five cards and the next card is turned up to set trumps. Each trick is worth 5. The first player to 25 wins at once, even mid hand.

    Trumps, strongest first

    The ace of hearts is always the third best trump. After the queen it is highest in red, lowest in black.

    Playing a trick

    • When a plain suit is led, follow suit or play a trump. With none of the suit, play anything.
    • When a trump is led, you must play a trump if you have one.
    • The five, the knave and the ace of hearts can be held back when a lower trump is led. A higher one forces them out.
    • The best trump wins. With no trump played, the best card of the suit led wins. The winner leads next.

    Robbing

    • If the turned card is an ace, the dealer may take it and throw away a card.
    • If you hold the ace of trumps, you may take the turned card on your first turn, before you play.

    Everyone gets three cards and three lives. One card is turned face up to start the discards. The last player with a life wins.

    What a hand counts

    • Add up your cards in one suit only. An ace is 11, a king, queen or knave is 10, the rest are their number.
    • The best hand is 31. Three of a kind counts 30½.

    Your turn

    • Take the top of the deck or the top discard, then throw one card away.
    • You cannot throw back a discard you have just picked up.
    • Or knock in place of drawing. Everyone else gets one more turn, then all hands are shown.

    Losing lives

    • The lowest hand loses a life. If hands tie for lowest they all lose one, but a knocker who ties is safe.
    • Make 31 and the round stops at once. Everyone else loses a life.
    • Out of lives, out of the game.
